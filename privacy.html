Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

Slate is desktop software made by Guerrilla Frame ("we"). This policy explains what data Slate and this website handle.

Your scripts and projects

Slate stores your scripts, projects, cast and crew details on your own computer. We do not receive, store or have access to them.

AI providers you choose

To analyse a script, Slate sends script text to the AI provider you select in Settings. If you choose a cloud provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek or OpenRouter), that provider receives the text under its own terms and privacy policy, using your own account and API key. If you choose a local model (Ollama or LM Studio), the text stays on your computer. Some providers' free tiers may use submitted data to improve their products; check their terms before sending unproduced material.

API keys

Your API keys are stored on your computer, encrypted by your operating system, and are sent only to the provider they belong to. We never receive them.

Licence activation

When you activate Slate, your licence key and a name for your computer are sent to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to check the licence. Slate may re-check the licence occasionally when you're online.

Weather and location lookups

If you turn on weather forecasts or look up a location's coordinates, Slate sends that location's coordinates or the place name you typed to Open-Meteo. No script content is sent.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. They receive your payment and billing details under their own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can provide your licence and support.
