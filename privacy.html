Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Slate is desktop software made by Guerrilla Frame ("we"). This policy explains what data Slate and this website handle.

Your scripts and projects

Slate stores your scripts, projects, cast and crew details on your own computer. We do not receive, store or have access to them.

AI providers you choose

To analyse a script, Slate sends script text to the AI provider you select in Settings. If you choose a cloud provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek or OpenRouter), that provider receives the text under its own terms and privacy policy, using your own account and API key. If you choose a local model (Ollama or LM Studio), the text stays on your computer. Some providers' free tiers may use submitted data to improve their products; check their terms before sending unproduced material.

API keys

Your API keys are stored on your computer, encrypted by your operating system, and are sent only to the provider they belong to. We never receive them.

Licence activation

When you activate Slate, your licence key and a name for your computer are sent to our payment provider, Polar, to check the licence. Slate may re-check the licence occasionally when you're online.

Weather and location lookups

If you turn on weather forecasts or look up a location's coordinates, Slate sends that location's coordinates or the place name you typed to Open-Meteo. No script content is sent.

Update checks

About once a day, Slate checks a small file on this website to see whether a newer version is available. The request contains no licence key, project details or other information about you; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the website's host (GitHub Pages). Slate never downloads or installs updates by itself. You can turn the daily check off in Settings.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Polar, which acts as the merchant of record. Polar receives your payment and billing details under their own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can provide your licence and support.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Email sign-ups

If you sign up for updates, we collect your email address to tell you about Slate's launch and new Guerrilla Frame tools. Sign-ups are delivered to us through Web3Forms. We don't sell or share your address, and you can ask us to remove it at any time by emailing guerrillaframe@gmail.com.
